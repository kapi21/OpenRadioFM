OPENRADIOFM

Your Radio, Your Rules

Customize your vehicle's instrumentation with low-level power. Extract firmware, audit RDS, and take total control. Optimized for 1024x600 screens and higher. 5.1.0 adds Backup Studio in the browser for favourites, options and full logo backups — installable as a PWA.

Download Application (APK) Technical Wiki

Premium Features

Extreme customization for your radio. Your style, your rules.

🎨

Custom Logos

Edit and manage station logos. Upload your own designs, car brand logos, and dynamic backgrounds with blur effect.

✏️

Advanced RDS Editing

Real-time RDS data decoding and editing. Customize station names and PS/RT text with maximum precision.

⭐

12 Favorites per Band

Save up to 12 favorite stations per FM band. Save/Load system to backup and share your lists (.fav).

💾

Backup Studio (5.1.0)

Back up favourites, options and full state with logos under RadioLogos. Edit in the browser; export .fav / .ors / .orzip and install as a PWA. Open from Save/Load or the online editor.

🌌

5 V.4 Themes

Choose from 5 premium typefaces: System, Bebas, Digital, Inter, and Orbitron. Day mode and night mode with automatic blue tint.

🗺️

Adaptive Layouts

Vertical (V2) and horizontal (V3) layouts optimized for 1024x600 screens. Glassmorphism design with premium effects.

☁️

Crowdsourcing Cloud

Contribute to the community. The app automatically uploads station data (PI Code/PS) so everyone has updated HD logos.

🌍

12 Languages

Interface and manuals in Spanish, English, Russian, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Romanian, Ukrainian, and more.

⚙️

Total Control

Low-level access for extreme customization. Premium menu with advanced aesthetic and functional settings.

🛠️

Hackable

Designed for enthusiasts who want to go beyond software. Open source and modifiable.

Hardware Specifications

  • Hardware: Compatible with MT8163, K706, and Radio NWD G5 (QS6) units.
  • Resolution: Optimized for 1024x600 screens and higher.
  • Radio Chip: NXP TEF6686 with full RDS support.
  • Firmware: Android 8.1+. Works with or without root (better performance with root).
